# What we have learned about the Smith and Hazleton family

Prepared September 14, 2026. This summary brings together the saved genealogy research and the newly archived Smith History Photos album. It is a synthesis of those materials, not a new verification of the underlying public records. The saved AI research reports are research notes; their citations should be checked before treating their claims as established genealogy.

## The most significant new finding

**Additional document discoveries:** Uncaptioned photo 032 is a July 19, 1895
funeral notice for James Louis Allan in Winchester, Kentucky; photo 036 is the
marker of Stanley Lee Haggard (June 11, 1898–April 17, 1969). Their family
relationships are not yet known. Photo 006 names the business Smiths Cleaners &
Dyers. See [transcriptions and follow-up leads](genealogy/photo-document-transcriptions.md).

**September 14 follow-up:** Direct photo review reads Susan A. Goode's marker as
**1855–1928**, contrary to the caption's 1923. A Waterloo Cemetery search-index
entry offers a candidate match for the Goode stones and a candidate identity for
the child in photo 024. John's death year and the child's relationship to Besse
remain disputed. See the [gravestone review](genealogy/gravestone-review.md) and
[structured family research](genealogy/README.md). Original captions remain intact.

The Photomyne album identifies **Myrtle Frances Smith Hazleton as the daughter of O.E. Smith Sr. and Besse Smith** (photo 009). Other captions identify Besse as **Besse M. Goode Smith**. Taken together with the earlier research identifying Myrtle as Jared's mother, these captions connect Jared's maternal family to the **Smith and Goode families**.

This updates the earlier genealogy report, which stopped at Alfred and Myrtle and could not establish Myrtle's maiden surname. We now have explicit family-caption evidence for Smith, although a marriage record, obituary, or birth certificate is still needed to corroborate that connection independently. [Sources: genealogy report, “Generation One”; album photos 004, 009, 010.]

## The family outline

The saved research identifies **Alfred Larson Hazleton and Myrtle Francis/Frances** as Jared's parents, and **Susan Swingen** as his sister. The album supplies the fuller name **Myrtle Frances Smith Hazleton** and identifies Susan as **Susan Hazleton Swingen** (002).

The combined working family outline is:

```text
O.E. Smith Sr. + Besse M. Goode Smith
              |
    Myrtle Frances Smith + Alfred Larson Hazleton
                         |
                         +-- Jared Earl Hazleton + Elaine
                         |                       |
                         |                       +-- Frances
                         |                       +-- Alan
                         |                       +-- Mark
                         |
                         +-- Susan Hazleton Swingen
```

The Smith-to-Myrtle connection comes from album captions. The immediate Hazleton relationships come from the obituary-based research notes. This outline does not list every sibling or descendant and should not be read as a complete family tree.

The research records Jared's birth as **September 12, 1937, in Oklahoma City**, and his death as **September 3, 2026**. It identifies Elaine as his wife of 66 years and names five grandchildren: **Weston, Kendall, Marlis, Berit, and Ian**. The cited obituary does not establish which child is the parent of each grandchild, so those connections remain unassigned here. [Source: genealogy report, “The documented family tree.”]

## The Smith family: relatives, businesses, and gatherings

The photographs preserve a family network across several generations. A Thanksgiving caption, dated approximately to the 1950s, names **O.E. Smith Jr., Al Hazleton, O.E. Smith Sr., and Jared Hazleton** together (003). Other photographs place Myrtle, Susan, Besse, and O.E. Smith Sr. together (011), and identify **Jared and Jackie Smith Devore as cousins** in the 1940s (019).

The captions describe the Smith family as operating **cleaning shops**. One image shows a shop whose location is unknown (006). Another caption says Besse and O.E. Smith lived in **Carthage** while O.E. helped his son convert an abandoned service station into a dry-cleaning shop (017). That caption tentatively places the photograph in Missouri; the photographed location is not established.

**Mills Smith** is identified as O.E. Smith Sr.'s younger brother and O.E. Smith Jr.'s uncle. According to the caption, Mills and O.E. Jr. were close in age, became friends, and shared an apartment in **Blackwell, Oklahoma, during the 1930s** (013). Another image identifies **O.E. Smith with his brother Kirby Smith** (039). The captions' playful references to the “infamous Smith Brothers” are family storytelling, not independently established characterizations.

Several captions discuss a pioneer cemetery near **Edmond, Oklahoma**. They propose burials for earlier Smith and Goode relatives, but use uncertain language and give differing distance descriptions (021, 035). Another caption identifies Besse beside O.E. ## Jared and Elaine's family story

The saved research describes Jared and Elaine meeting at the **University of Oklahoma** and building a family through his Navy service, graduate education, and economics career. It cites a Bedford, Massachusetts, municipal record naming Jared and Elaine as Mark's parents, giving a specific documentary lead for the family's Massachusetts years.

Jared's career subsequently included Texas, an assignment in **Amman, Jordan**, and Washington State. Those appointments provide a framework for organizing family memories, but the professional chronology alone does not establish where every family member lived at each point. [Sources: genealogy report; ChatGPT-Research-Web.md.]

The album adds photographs captioned **“Jared and Elaine Hazleton family wedding”** (026–027) and a family photograph (028). The wording does not establish whose wedding is pictured, so these should not yet be labeled as Jared and Elaine's own wedding photographs.

## Questions and contradictions to resolve

- **Elizabeth Goode's relationship to Jared:** Photo 001 calls Jared her grandson; photo 020 names them together without a relationship. The album does not establish Elizabeth's connection to W.H. Goode. Do not assume she was W.H.'s wife: combining that assumption with the Besse–Myrtle–Jared line would place her a generation farther back than “grandmother.”
- **Elaine's maiden name and wedding date:** The Claude report states **Elaine Upton, November 27, 1959**. The ChatGPT genealogy report says neither was established and estimates approximately 1960 from the marriage duration. A 1959 wedding is consistent with 66 completed years by September 2026, but that arithmetic does not verify the exact date or maiden name.
- **Name variations:** Sources use Hazleton/Hazelton, Frances/Francis, and Besse/Bessie, with additional variations in Besse's middle name. Preserve original spellings and search the variants; do not silently treat every variation as a separate person or an established correction.
- **Tentative photo identifications:** Photos 012, 014, and 038 explicitly express uncertainty. Photo 024 offers a tentative reading of a child's birth and death dates. Photos 032, 034, and 036 have no captions.
- **Alfred's ancestry:** The new album substantially expands the maternal story but does not establish Alfred Larson Hazleton's parents or earlier paternal ancestry.
- **Source chronology:** The Claude report describes a September 19, 2026 celebration as already held, although this summary is dated September 14. That wording should not be repeated as a completed event.

The most useful next records are **Alfred and Myrtle's marriage record**, **Myrtle's obituary or death certificate**, census records linking the Smith and Goode households, and cemetery records matching the photographed graves. Family confirmation of the album's author and the uncertain identifications would also strengthen its value as a source.
