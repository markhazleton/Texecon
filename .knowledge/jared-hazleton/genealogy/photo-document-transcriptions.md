# Newly read documents and image clues

Manual visual review, September 14, 2026. These notes supplement the original captions, which remain unchanged. An uncaptioned image can still contain readable documentary evidence.

## Photo 032 — James Louis Allan funeral notice

[Original image](../photomyne-smith-history/images/032.jpg)

Transcription below joins words split across printed lines; punctuation and names follow the image:

> Allan.
>
> The funeral and interment of James Louis Allan will take place at the Winchester cemetery, to-morrow, Saturday, at three o'clock, P. M. Services conducted by Rev. B. B. Bailey, and interment in charge of Winchester Lodge No. 20, F. and A. M.
>
> PALL BEARERS:
>
> J. W. POYNTER. — J. A. RAMSEY.
>
> J. B. PHARIS. — M. T. McDONALD.
>
> PHIL. HODGKIN. — J. C. VAUGHT.
>
> Winchester, Ky., July 19th, 1895.

The notice names **James Louis Allan**, gives its issue place as **Winchester, Kentucky**, and dates itself **July 19, 1895**. It schedules funeral and interment for the next day, yielding **July 20, 1895, at 3 p.m.** as a calculated scheduled event. This is not by itself proof that the service occurred or that July 19 was the date of death. His connection to the Smith/Hazleton family is unknown.

A targeted search for `"James Louis Allan" Winchester` returned a [Clark County Public Library-hosted obituary compilation](https://www.clarkbooks.org/_files/ugd/07c490_14000e97f1974f1e85e14cddc20c76e1.pdf). Its search excerpt attributes an obituary to **The Winchester Democrat, July 23, 1895**, reports death on a Friday in Allansville at age 52, and names parents **Judge Frank S. and Elizabeth Allan**. The PDF entry and underlying newspaper page have not been inspected, so these remain candidate details and are not established parent-child links in the ledger.

**Next:** inspect the compilation entry and obtain the July 23 newspaper page; check the cemetery record; then determine why the notice was preserved in this album. Do not attach James to the family solely because the notice is in the collection.

This strengthens the Waterloo location hypothesis for at least part of the album's cemetery material. The photograph contains no cemetery sign, and the index's linked photo has not been inspected. His relationship to the family is unknown.

**Next:** obtain his obituary or a census household with named relatives, and compare the index's grave photo when accessible. His surname should not be treated as evidence of a new ancestral branch yet.

## Photo 006 — business name

[Original image](../photomyne-smith-history/images/006.jpg)

The storefront sign reads **SMITHS CLEANERS & DYERS**. This adds an exact business-name search clue to the caption's general reference to family cleaning shops. The sign does not identify an owner, city, or date. Do not assign it to Carthage or Blackwell simply because those towns occur in other captions.

Search variants to try in city directories and newspapers: `Smiths Cleaners`, `Smith's Cleaners`, `Smith Cleaners`, and `Cleaners & Dyers`, combined with the already documented family towns. Initial web searches combining the business with Carthage or Blackwell did not establish a match.
