## Evidence conventions

`source_checked` means a source was read and supports the stated claim; it does not mean all facts in that source are independently proven. `caption_reported` preserves family album testimony. `prior_report_only` identifies claims whose underlying sources have not been checked in this research pass. `visual_reading` records an observation from an image. `candidate` is a possible match. `disputed` preserves conflicting evidence without resolving it automatically.

Person names are working display labels, not assertions that every part of a name has been legally verified. Photo tags refer to caption identifications, not facial recognition. A person appearing beside another person does not establish a relationship. Unnamed or ambiguous people are not merged into named relatives.

The JSON is a research ledger, not GEDCOM and not yet a complete tree. Claims may express grandparents or siblings directly without inventing missing parent-child links. Preserve source IDs and person IDs when extending it. Add a claim and its evidence before promoting a tentative connection into an accepted tree.
