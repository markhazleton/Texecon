# Gravestone evidence review

Reviewed September 14, 2026 directly from the archived image files. These are manual visual readings, not OCR or visits to the cemetery. Original files and captions have not been altered.

## Photo 035 — John and Susan A. Goode

[Open original](../photomyne-smith-history/images/035.jpg) · [Original caption](../photomyne-smith-history/catalog.md#photo-035)

The shared marker clearly reads **GOODE**, **SUSAN A.**, and **1855–1928** on the left. The right reads **JOHN**, with birth year **1844**. John's death year is less clear in this photograph; the last digit should not be resolved from the caption alone.

The caption gives Susan's death year as **1923**, so the image itself provides a reason to revise that working date to **1928**, while preserving the caption's original wording. It gives John's death year as **1925**.

A search-index result for [Waterloo Cemetery, Oklahoma County](https://www.okcemeteries.net/oklahoma/waterloo/waterloo.htm), inspected September 14, lists Susan A. Goode as **1855–1928**, wife of John, and John Goode as **1844–1923**. This supplies a promising cemetery match and an additional disagreement over John's death year. The full page returned HTTP 429, so its linked photographs were not inspected and no cemetery-register entry was obtained.

Working assessment: Susan's **inscription** supports 1928 with high visual confidence. John's death year remains disputed (caption 1925 versus index 1923). Waterloo is a candidate identification for this marker, not a confirmed location for every cemetery photograph in the album. The marker does not itself establish that this couple were Besse's grandparents.

## Photo 024 — child's marker

[Open original](../photomyne-smith-history/images/024.jpg) · [Original caption](../photomyne-smith-history/catalog.md#photo-024)

The stone is weathered. The name appears to include **BRYAN**, followed by a relationship line and the surname **GOODE**. The years appear consistent with **1897** and **1900**, but a complete independent transcription of the name, parents' initials, and exact dates is not secure from this image.

The same Waterloo search result lists **William J. Bryan Goode**, May 23, 1897–May 10, 1900, as a son of **J. and S. A. Goode**. Treat this as a candidate match, not a verified transcription of photo 024. The exact indexed dates must be checked against the linked photograph or another record.

The caption calls the child Besse's baby brother. If the index's parents are John and Susan A., and photo 035's caption correctly identifies them as Besse's paternal grandparents, the child would instead be in Besse's father's generation. This is a conditional conflict, not proof of the child's identity or of either relationship. Do not resolve it by silently moving a generation in the tree.

## Photo 023 — woman beside a grave

[Open original](../photomyne-smith-history/images/023.jpg) · [Original caption](../photomyne-smith-history/catalog.md#photo-023)

The image shows a woman beside a low grave marker. The caption identifies her as Besse and the grave as O.E. Smith's. The marker's lettering is too small and oblique for a reliable name/date transcription in this review. Neither a full name for O.E. nor a cemetery identity can be established visually here.

The caption's approximate 1960s date and statement that Besse was later buried beside O.E. remain caption testimony only.
