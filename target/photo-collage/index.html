Collage Studio

Turn a folder of photographs into a story worth watching.

Create cinematic photo collages, add music or narration, preview motion, and render a finished MP4 with the local Windows app.

How to make your first collage

  1. Install and open the app from the Windows Start Menu.
  2. Open or create a JSON project.
  3. Select image and optional audio folders.
  4. Edit scenes, timing, transitions, captions, and motion.
  5. Render the finished MP4 and open the output.

What you need

Windows 10 or 11 on a 64-bit computer, photographs in JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP format, and optional audio in MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, or FLAC format.

Download for Windows

Download CollageStudioSetup.exe

Your photos, projects, and renders stay on your computer. No account or media upload is required.
